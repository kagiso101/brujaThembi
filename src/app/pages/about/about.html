"Breaking free from those painful patterns equipped me with invaluable tools for transformation."

Her path led her to the study of energy, astrology, and the teachings of Dr. Credo Mutwa — a renowned Sanusi — alongside the work of Dolores Cannon. Theory became practice. She manifested her first R20K using saturation processes, and soon was averaging R20K months doing energy work online.
