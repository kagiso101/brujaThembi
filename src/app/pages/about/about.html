Her Story
The Beginning

From Stuck
to Standing On Her Own

Thembi Molaba began card readings vocationally in 2018, going full-time after COVID in 2021. But her story starts long before. Over a decade ago, she battled clinical depression — unemployable and trapped in a cycle of stagnation. What she calls a "mindset of lack and separation" was the root of it all.

"Breaking free from those painful patterns equipped me with invaluable tools for transformation."

That took her to the study of energy, astrology, and the teachings of Dr. Credo Mutwa — a renowned Sanusi. Theory turned into practice. She brought in her first R20,000 doing this work, and before long she was averaging R20,000 months reading for people online.
